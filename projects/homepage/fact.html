Shark facts

Quick facts

  • Sharks do not hunt humans in any capacity.
  • Sharks are incredibly curious creatures.
  • Sharks have existed longer than trees.

Myths vs. reality

Sharks do not hunt humans intentionally. While 5-10 shark attacks occur yearly, which can occur for a variety of reasons, most scuba divers report being ignored by sharks.
