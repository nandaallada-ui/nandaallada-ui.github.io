Shark subspecies

Basking shark

  • Lifespan: 50 years
  • Size: 7 to 8.5 meters, 20-23 feet
  • Habitat: Costal and Ocean waters

Lemon shark

  • Lifespan: 25-30 years
  • Size: 3 meters, 8-12 feet
  • Habitat: Shallow and coastal waters

Great White shark

  • Lifespan: 70 years
  • Size: 11 - 21 feet, 3-6 meters
  • Habitat: Temperate and tropical offshore waters
